# Support

Cubeulator is made by one person, so there is no guaranteed reply time, and there is no support email address. Help comes
in this order.

## 1. The guide

- [Troubleshooting](guide/troubleshooting.md) goes through what the screen says and what to do about it: the camera will
  not open, a side will not read, the six sides do not make a cube, the solution does not match your cube.
- [Scanning a cube](guide/scan.md) says how to hold the cube and what each message means.
- The [FAQ](faq.md) answers the common questions.

## 2. A GitHub issue

If the guide does not settle it, or a cube will not read, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/iainchesworthlabs/cubeulator/issues/new/choose). It needs a free GitHub account. The most
useful ones say:

- the **version** (*Settings*, then *About Cubeulator*, or the application menu on a Mac) and your **system**, or the
  browser and its version;
- the **camera**, and the kind of **light** the cube was in;
- what the screen **said**, word for word;
- what the **cube** looks like: its body colour and sticker style.

Please do not attach a picture that shows a person or the room behind them.

## 3. A security matter

Report it **privately**, not as a public issue, through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/iainchesworthlabs/cubeulator/security/advisories/new). The
[security policy](https://github.com/iainchesworthlabs/cubeulator/blob/main/docs/SECURITY.md) says what counts and what to
expect. If you cannot use the form, open an issue that says only that you have a security matter, with no details, and the
author will reply there to arrange a private channel.

## Not sure a download is genuine?

[Verifying a download](guide/verify.md) shows how to check a package against its build attestation and the author's
signature.

## Contributing

Code, translations and bug reports are welcome. The branching model and how a pull request is reviewed are in the
[contributing guide](https://github.com/iainchesworthlabs/cubeulator/blob/develop/docs/CONTRIBUTING.md) on GitHub.
