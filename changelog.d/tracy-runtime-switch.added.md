**Profiling can be turned on in a shipped build, without a recompile.** The Tracy profiler is built into the desktop applications and stays dormant - no thread, no socket, a nanosecond per instrumented call - until the application is started with `--profile` or with `CUBEULATOR_PROFILE=1` in its environment (`--no-profile` / `CUBEULATOR_PROFILE=0` the other way). A Debug build profiles from launch with no flag. The profiler listens on the loopback interface only. How to switch it on, capture and read a trace, and what it costs idle, is in [Profiling](docs/profiling.md#turning-it-on).
