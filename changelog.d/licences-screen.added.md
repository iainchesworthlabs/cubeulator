**About and Licences are real screens.** **Settings > About Cubeulator** (and the Help menu on a Mac) now opens an About screen with the name, the version, who made it, the licence Cubeulator is under and the address of its source code, instead of a small dialog, and under it **Licences** lists Cubeulator and every third-party component that is in your build, the bundled typefaces (Archivo and IBM Plex Mono) among them, each opening to its full copyright notice and licence. The notices are the ones the build generated, so they cannot drift from what ships; they stay in their authors' words in every language, set left to right, and can be selected, copied and scrolled with the keyboard. In all nine languages, with the layout mirrored for Hebrew, Arabic and Yiddish. See [Settings](docs/guide/settings.md#about-and-licences).
