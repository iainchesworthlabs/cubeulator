**Buttons and choices that are switched off can be read, in the light and dark themes alike.** They used to be faded, and the Slow, Normal and Fast chips under "Auto-rotate when idle" were faded twice, once as a row and once each, so their words were hard to make out when it is off, and a switched-off button that was red or filled hardly showed at all. Every one is now a plain grey button with a clear outline, whatever its kind and wherever it is, Start over on the scan screen included; the choice that is selected keeps a second ring inside its outline, so you can still tell which one it is; and the chips are whole again when Auto-rotate is on.
