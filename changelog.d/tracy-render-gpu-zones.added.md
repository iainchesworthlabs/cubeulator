**The render thread is profiled too.** Each Qt Quick presentation is broken into CPU zones on the scene graph's render thread (the synchronisation, the whole RHI frame, the render and its main pass) and, on Vulkan, Direct3D 11 and OpenGL, a GPU zone that times the GPU executing that pass on the graphics timeline; plots of the presentation and camera-frame cadences let a capture show how often frames actually arrive, and Qt's own timing of the scene graph (the swap, the renderer's preprocess where the first frame builds its shaders, glyph and texture uploads) is plotted beside them. Direct3D 12, Metal and software rendering keep the CPU zones and plots but have no GPU zone. See [Profiling](docs/profiling.md#the-render-pass-cpu-and-gpu).
