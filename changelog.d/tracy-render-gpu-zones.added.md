**The render thread is profiled too.** Each Qt Quick presentation is a CPU zone on the scene graph's render thread and, on OpenGL and Direct3D 11, a GPU zone that brackets the frame's draw calls on the graphics timeline; plots of the presentation and camera-frame cadences let a capture show how often frames actually arrive. Direct3D 12, Vulkan, Metal and software rendering keep the CPU zone and plot but have no GPU zone yet.
