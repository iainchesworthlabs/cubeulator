**Screen readers are given names, roles and headings, and told what changes.** Every screen has a name and a heading; buttons, choices and switches say what they are and whether they are on or chosen; the cube on the solve screen is described by the move to make; the picture and the six chips of the scan have names. The scan's guidance, each move of the solve with its place in the solve, the turns of the solution, a mistake in a typed scramble and a deleted solve are announced as they happen. This is checked by automated tests of the app's accessibility information and keyboard; it has not yet been tried with a screen reader. See [Accessibility](docs/accessibility.md).
