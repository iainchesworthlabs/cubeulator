**Every word on every screen is now in the language you chose.** The scan screen, the solve screen (the stage names and what each asks, what the camera says about your cube, the note when a turn goes the wrong way, **Previous**, **Out of step? Rescan**), the finishing screen, the developer rows in Settings and the colours screen's lines about turning a side that was read the wrong way up were English whatever the language. They are now in Deutsch, Français, Español, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Hebrew, Arabic and Yiddish. These are still first drafts that no fluent reader has been through ([Settings](docs/guide/settings.md#language)). In English a few words change so that one screen no longer says what another says differently: the solve screen names its stages and says what each asks as the solution screen does (*Yellow top*, *Top layer in order*), the move reads *Left* and *Right*, the camera's note says to press *Next turn*, and the scan says *Reading this side. Hold it there*.
