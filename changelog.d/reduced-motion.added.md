**The app moves less when you ask it to.** Settings has a **Motion** row (the same as this device, reduced, or full), and by default the app follows the device's own request for less motion where Qt can read it, so a device that has not asked sees no change. Reduced stops the start screen's cube turning by itself, draws the layer to turn leaning and still instead of swinging, makes turns snap, and takes out the bobbing cube and the pulses and ripples of the scan and solved screens. See [Accessibility](docs/accessibility.md).
