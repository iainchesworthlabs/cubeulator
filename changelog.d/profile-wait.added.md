**A profiler can be waited for, so start-up is in the trace.** Started with `--profile-wait` (or `CUBEULATOR_PROFILE=wait`), the application starts its profiler and holds, before it builds anything, until a Tracy profiler connects - for at most 30 seconds, after which it carries on as usual. A capture started by hand after launch misses start-up; this one does not. See [Profiling](docs/profiling.md#capturing-the-start-up).
