**Every button can be reached and pressed with the keyboard.** <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves between the buttons, choices and switches, and the rows of History, About and Licences (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> goes back); <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> presses the one you are on, and a ring of two bands shows where you are. Until now only About, Licences and a few text boxes took the keyboard. On the colours screen the stickers of a side are one stop: the arrow keys move over them, <kbd>Space</kbd> paints, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> clears, <kbd>1</kbd> to <kbd>6</kbd> choose the colour and <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> change side. A click or a tap does not move keyboard focus, so the solve screen's keys work as they did. See [Accessibility](docs/accessibility.md).
