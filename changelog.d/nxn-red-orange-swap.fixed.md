**A 4x4 (or any even size) is no longer scanned with red and orange swapped.** A cube with no centre stickers has nothing to say which colour is which, so the scan named red and orange by how close each looked to a reference colour, and in some light (a lamp or a window that shifts the greens, say) it named them the wrong way round - and then built a complete, valid cube out of it, with every red sticker orange. The scan now also checks the cube itself: the colours of a real cube go round a corner the same way on every cube, and swapping two names turns that into its mirror image, so a naming that does not make a real cube's colours is corrected before the cube is shown. 2x2, 4x4 and 6x6 are covered; a 3x3, 5x5 and 7x7 were already protected by their centre stickers.
