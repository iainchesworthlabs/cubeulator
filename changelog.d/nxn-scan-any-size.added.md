**Scan a cube of another size with the camera.** *Another size of cube* now has a Scan button at every size from 2×2 to 5×5, and the scan screen has size buttons (2×2 to 5×5) and an **Auto** button. With nothing chosen the scan works the size out from what the camera sees, until the first side is captured; choose a size and it stays until you press Auto. A cube held at a corner is read at every size offered, as at 3×3. From the solve screen, *Out of step? Rescan* is there for every size and starts the new scan at the size of the cube you are solving. The camera has been tried on cubes drawn on a screen and on a few recorded sessions of a 4×4 and a 5×5, not yet on many cubes in a hand: **Check the colours** puts right what it misreads.
