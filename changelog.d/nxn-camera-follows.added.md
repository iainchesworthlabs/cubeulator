**The camera follows a bigger cube as it does a 3x3.** On the solve screen of a 2x2, 4x4 or 5x5, hold a side square to the camera and the cube on screen turns to match how you hold it; make the turn asked for in view and the solution steps on by itself; turn the wrong side and it says which. A layer the side you are showing does not touch (one behind the front layer, say) cannot be seen, and the screen says so and waits for **Next turn**, as it does whenever the camera is not sure: a wrong reading never steps the solution. Tried on drawn cubes and on real holds of a 2x2, 4x4 and 5x5, not yet on a real bigger cube being turned. See [docs/nxn-camera-follow.md](docs/nxn-camera-follow.md).
