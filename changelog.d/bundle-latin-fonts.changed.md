**The app carries its own type, and starts faster in the Latin-script languages.** Archivo and IBM Plex Mono, the faces the design names, are now part of the app on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS (the web build is unchanged), so English, German, French and Spanish text is set in them on every machine, where a machine without them showed Tahoma or whatever its own default was. Looking for the two families it did not have cost about a third of a second at start-up: the first screen now comes up roughly 200 ms sooner in those languages. Japanese, Chinese, Hebrew, Arabic and Yiddish text is drawn in the same faces as before. Their licences are in the third-party notices. See [Performance](docs/performance.md#the-fonts).
